Doodh Hisab App for Daily Milk Records

Aarish Dairy Pro is a free doodh hisab app for keeping daily milk records, customer entries and monthly dairy hisab in one place.

Why doodh hisab needs a digital record

Many dairy sellers still keep milk records in a paper diary. Paper records can be lost, damaged or hard to calculate at month end. Aarish Dairy Pro gives a simple digital way to record daily milk quantity, rate and customer details.

This page helps users who search for doodh hisab, doodh ka hisab, dairy hisab kitab, milk record app or daily milk collection record.

India and international use

Aarish Dairy Pro can be used across India and internationally by Hindi or English-speaking users who need a simple dairy record, milk record or daily hisab app.

How Aarish Dairy Pro helps

  • Add milk customers and keep customer-wise records.
  • Record daily milk quantity and rate.
  • Check monthly milk hisab in a clean mobile interface.
  • Use the same app with credit book, udhar khata book, expenses and salary register.
