Milk Record App for Dairy Collection and Monthly Hisab

Aarish Dairy Pro is a milk record app for daily milk collection, dairy customer records, monthly hisab and small dairy business tracking.

Daily milk collection record

A milk record app should be simple, fast and mobile-friendly. Aarish Dairy Pro is made for small dairy and milk selling users who want to keep customer-wise milk records and business hisab without complicated accounting software.
